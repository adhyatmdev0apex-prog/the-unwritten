# Character Bible

> Names are final: **Lucien** and **Elara**. Faces/designs are not yet locked. Do not generate scene footage until the reference packs in `characters/` are approved.

## LUCIEN
- **Age:** 16
- **Role:** the observer; protective by instinct, learning that Elara doesn't need protecting.
- **Look:** taller, dark hair, calm eyes. Old leather notebook he sketches in.
- **Tell:** touches his sleeve cuff when anxious.
- **In scenes:** moves her bag before it falls; helps her stand; turns his hand and lets her choose instead of telling her to hold on.

## ELARA
- **Age:** 16
- **Role:** sharp, brave, first to notice the impossible.
- **Look:** dark-teal scarf lining, a pencil always behind her ear.
- **Tell:** tilts her head when she's working something out.
- **In scenes:** takes his pen without asking; reaches for his hand first; slowly tests the bond by trying to let go.

## Relationship rules
1. Affection is small and habitual (hand-holding, sleeve-catching, a shared look). Never announced, no "I love you" in the competition cut.
2. Neither protects alone. If one steps in front, the other pulls them level.
3. Their love stays *theirs*. The sphere recognises a bond they already chose; it does not order them to love.
4. In every frame where both appear, show their hands when it matters. Hands are the story.

## Uniform (shared system)
Long tailored charcoal coat, dark-teal lining, minimal bronze fasteners, original geometric academy emblem, natural fabric folds. Lucien: structured silhouette. Elara: slightly more relaxed, teal scarf lining visible.

## Palette
Slate-teal shadows - 2700K golden magic - black-glass void. Magic is **gold only**.

## Performance rules
- Look toward what attracts attention; react to light and sound.
- Shift weight naturally; plausible personal space; few hand gestures.
- Pause when overwhelmed. For the academy reveal, near-motionless: the world moves, they stare.

## Supporting
- **The Creature:** unfinished ink creature, featureless face, feathered wings; walks them to the sealed door and collapses into ink.
- **The Being / Thing Behind the Sphere:** never shown whole.
- **Academy staff (movie only):** a headmistress figure (working name Orven Selke) who may believe the school's lie. Optional.
