# Character Bible — THE UNWRITTEN

> **Status:** Names and Relationship Direction **B** are locked. Reference images (`CHAR-01` Lucien, `CHAR-02` Elara, `CHAR-03` Hands/Mark) must be generated and approved in Stage 1 **before** any scene footage is produced.

---

## 1. Canonical Relationship Direction: B (Drawn Together Without Knowing Why)
- Lucien and Elara feel an inexplicable connection, but neither understands its origin.
- They are comfortable around each other, naturally curious about one another, and instinctively protective.
- **Crucial Distinction:** We do **not** establish that they are already in love in the short film (`1:50`) or competition cut (`58s`). B does not mean they feel nothing; it means they don't yet understand what they feel—or why.
- Their bond develops through choices: in Scene 5, when Elara reaches out and hesitates, **Lucien deliberately chooses to take her hand** (*"Whatever that thing is… we face it together."*).

## 2. LUCIEN
- **Age:** ~16 years old
- **Physical Appearance:** Tall and lean, dark tousled hair, observant dark eyes, quiet and thoughtful expression. Natural skin texture, photorealistic live-action look.
- **Costume:**
  - Tailored charcoal school coat with subtle dark-teal inner lining and minimal aged-bronze fasteners.
  - Simple dark school uniform underneath.
  - Carries an old leather notebook throughout the film.
- **Personality & Performance:** Observant, reserved, intelligent, quietly protective. Understated expressions—no melodrama.
- **Reference Pack Location:** [`characters/lucien/`](../characters/lucien/README.md) (6-view sheet: front, 3/4, side profile, full-body neutral, uncertainty close-up, quiet determination close-up).

## 3. ELARA
- **Age:** ~16 years old
- **Physical Appearance:** Intelligent, observant expression, natural and understated appearance (a believable student, not a heavily stylized fantasy heroine).
- **Costume:**
  - Tailored charcoal school coat with visible dark-teal scarf lining.
  - Simple dark school uniform underneath.
  - Small hexagonal wooden pencil tucked behind her ear.
- **Personality & Performance:** Curious, perceptive, quietly courageous. First to tease Lucien in the classroom; first to reach toward the sphere and toward Lucien's hand in the chamber.
- **Reference Pack Location:** [`characters/elara/`](../characters/elara/README.md) (7-view sheet: front, 3/4, side profile, full-body neutral, curiosity close-up, uncertainty close-up, resolve close-up).

## 4. Supernatural Entities & Symbols
- **The Golden Palm Marks:** A single thin molten-gold line across each palm (`S04-SH04`) that connects into one continuous interwoven symbol when Lucien takes Elara's hand (`S05-SH02`). Must use the exact same design in every shot.
- **The Unfinished Creature (Scene 3):** Drawn in black ink on old parchment; ends abruptly at the chest; featureless face; emerges into 3D space dripping ink, reveals a single thin golden line across its body, and leads them to the sealed door.
- **The Entity Behind the Sphere (Scenes 4 & 5):** Concealed in the vast darkness behind the black-glass sphere. Never shown as a full monster—only an enormous shifting silhouette (`S04-SH06`) and a single colossal golden eye opening in the dark (`S05-SH05`).
