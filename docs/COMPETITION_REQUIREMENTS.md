## Deliverable
- AI video reel, **9:16 vertical**, **45-60 seconds** (target 58 s)
- Resolution 1080x1920, MP4 or MOV
- File size: the portal shows both "under 250 MB" and "Max 100 MB" - **stay under 100 MB** to be safe
- Uploaded through the Creator Studio portal (streamed to Google Drive)

## Portal fields
1. **Project / Reel Title** (required)
2. **Video file** (required)
3. **AI tools used:** Select the tools actually used from the portal checklist (Runway, Midjourney, ElevenLabs, ChatGPT, Claude, Sora, Suno, Udio, Adobe Firefly). *(Note: Pika and CapCut are strictly not used; our production deploys Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash / Veo alongside professional DaVinci Resolve Studio & FFmpeg encoding pipelines.)*
4. **Concept, storyline and prompt log** (optional but valuable)

## What the judges compare against
Benchmarks: The Lion King, Spirited Away, Spider-Verse, WALL-E, GdT Pinocchio, Ne Zha 2, Inside Out 2, Zootopia 2, Puss in Boots: The Last Wish. Aim for cinematic realism, strong story and emotion, and effects of feature-film quality.

## Honest rules to follow
- No AI tools are provided; teams use their own and list them.
- Be accurate in the tool list and prompt log (only list tools you actually used).
- Original characters and world; don't copy protected designs.
