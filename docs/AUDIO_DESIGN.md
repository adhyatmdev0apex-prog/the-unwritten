# Audio Design

**No narrator anywhere.** Sound and music carry the film. The only spoken words are Lucien and Elara's few lines plus engraved/on-screen inscriptions.

## Five layers
1. **Ambience:** classroom room tone, fluorescent hum, faint corridor footsteps, pencil on wood.
2. **Low-frequency tension:** a restrained drone that grows slowly; sub-bass under the anomaly.
3. **Magical sound identity:** crystalline ice-crack when gold appears, low bronze resonance, a slow heartbeat pulse.
4. **Music:** none until the academy reveal. Then solo cello into choir and horns. Two motifs, one per lead, that join only when their marks align.
5. **Silence:** the sphere chamber, the moment before the title.

## Mix rules
Dialogue stays intelligible over music; no clipping; no abrupt discontinuities; keep the sudden silences deliberate.
