# Audio & Sound Design — THE UNWRITTEN

**Core Rule:** No narrator or voice-over anywhere in the film. Do not ask video generators to synthesize final spoken dialogue, music, and sound effects inside video clips—build the soundscape across six independent tracks (`A1`–`A6`) in post-production.

---

## 1. Six-Track Audio Layout

| Track | Content | Production Method |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | School ambience & bell | Recorded or licensed classroom room tone, paper rustling, school bell (`0:04`), chairs scraping, footsteps |
| **A2** | Lucien's dialogue | Recorded voice or permitted AI voice generator (7 lines in 1:50 cut; 4 lines in 58s cut) |
| **A3** | Elara's dialogue | Recorded voice or permitted AI voice generator (8 lines in 1:50 cut; 3 lines in 58s cut) |
| **A4** | Supernatural & physical SFX | Rolling pencil stop, kintsugi crack, floor fracture, wind, basalt landing, ink drip/scrape, bronze rings, heartbeat, 20Hz sub-bass, final impact |
| **A5** | Musical score | Original composition or properly licensed generated music (see Master Music Prompt below) |
| **A6** | Final stereo mix | Balanced in DaVinci Resolve; dialogue clear over score; zero clipping |

---

## 2. Scene-by-Scene Sonic Progression (1:50 Canonical Timeline)

- **Scene 1 — The First Fracture (0:00–0:20):**
  - `0:00–0:10`: Warm classroom ambience, school bell rings, chairs scrape, students depart.
  - `0:10–0:15`: Quiet afternoon room tone under Elara and Lucien's conversation (`Lines 1–3`).
  - `0:15–0:20`: Faint metallic hum rises beneath the desks; wooden pencil roll stops abruptly; crystalline kintsugi crack; `Lines 4–5` (*"Lucien…?" / "Stay close."*); deep supernatural impact on cut to black.
- **Scene 2 — The World Beyond (0:20–0:40):**
  - `0:20–0:33`: Rushing high-altitude wind, falling debris, distorted spatial echoes, solid basalt landing.
  - `0:33–0:40`: Powerful orchestral reveal (`A5`: low brass, strings, distant choir) as the Gothic academy rises and the central doors groan open (`Lines 6–7`).
- **Scene 3 — The Forbidden Classroom (0:40–1:00):**
  - Paper scraping, wet ink dripping as the unfinished creature lifts from the page (`Lines 8–9`), faint golden harmonic chime, and distant heavy metallic movement behind the sealed door (`Lines 10–11`).
- **Scene 4 — The Chamber That Remembers (1:00–1:25):**
  - Music drops to sparse low drones and delicate metallic harmonics; rotating bronze rings decelerate to near-total silence.
  - Faint heartbeat pulse under `Lines 12–14`; when the golden light extinguishes at `1:21`, drop into dead silence followed by a low, inhuman sub-bass rumble.
- **Scene 5 — Something Woke Up (1:25–1:50):**
  - Subtle, emotionally resonant motif enters at `1:29` when Lucien deliberately takes Elara's hand (`Line 15`).
  - Rising orchestral tension as golden lines race through the academy and the dome stops (`1:35–1:42`).
  - Sudden drop to near-total silence as the entity opens its glowing golden eye (`1:42–1:46`), punctuated by one deep cinematic impact on the smash cut to black (`1:46`).
