## Chapter-by-Chapter Generation Prompts

### Chapter 1: After the Bell (0:00–1:30)
- **Classroom Interior:** `cinematic film still, high school classroom at dusk, tall rain-streaked windows, slate-blue twilight, wooden desks, two teenage students Lucien (16, dark hair, charcoal coat) and Elara (16, charcoal coat with dark-teal scarf lining) packing bags. Natural lighting, 35mm lens, photorealistic.`
- **Floor Anomaly:** `extreme macro shot beneath wooden desk, a patch of light-absorbing zero-reflection black void, a wooden pencil locked motionless at the edge, hair-thin molten gold kintsugi fissures branching across the darkness, warm amber rim-light.`
- **The Dimensional Fall:** `cinematic shot, Lucien and Elara falling in free fall past an upside-down pine forest floating in the sky, drifting stone Gothic arches, and suspended ocean water, clothes whipping in wind, dramatic atmospheric depth.`

### Chapter 2: Meridian Arrival & The Academy (1:30–3:00)
- **Causeway Landing:** `cinematic establishing shot, Lucien and Elara standing on a vast wet black-basalt causeway suspended high above rolling clouds, looking up at an immense Gothic citadel with floating arched bridges, rotating bronze astrolabes, and teal stained glass.`
- **The Great Inscription:** `macro architectural detail of a colossal Gothic stone archway, engraved in weathered stone with glowing molten-gold inlay: WELCOME TO YOUR REAL SCHOOL. Mist drifting past ancient carvings.`
- **Teal-Lined Academy Students:** `medium shot of academy corridors, students in long charcoal coats with dark-teal lining walking across slender suspended stone bridges between towers, impossible vertical spaces.`

### Chapter 3: The Curriculum (3:00–4:30)
- **Mathematics (Architecture of Gravity):** `massive vaulted stone hall, a two-ton fractured granite boulder suspended weightlessly in mid-air, orbiting thin 3D golden calculus vectors and concentric brass rings at a student's hand gesture, realistic physical inertia.`
- **Science (Birth of Galaxies):** `dark amphitheater laboratory, hovering above a dark slate table is an optical refractive glass sphere containing a living rotating spiral galaxy with violet nebula clouds and thousands of pinpoint stars, colored reflections on student faces.`
- **History (Living Civilizations):** `ancient stone corridor where a colossal arched doorway opens directly into the midday sun and dust of an ancient Roman or Babylonian marble city, students in modern charcoal coats stepping through the threshold.`
- **Art (The Living Drawing):** `extreme macro on black vellum parchment, an anatomical sketch of a winged serpent in golden ink; the metallic ink scales lift into 3D space, a head rises off the paper, a living amber eye blinks, casting real shadows.`

### Chapter 4: The School's Rule & Separation (4:30–6:00)
- **Headmistress Orven Selke:** `portrait of a stern, regal academy headmistress in dark-teal velvet and bronze robes, standing before a high stained-glass window, expression of solemn dread, photorealistic portrait.`
- **Separated Towers:** `split vertical composition: Lucien alone in a high library tower looking out at distant towers; Elara alone in a shadowed astronomy spire; between them, impossible moving staircases detaching and repositioning across the sky.`

### Chapter 5: The Subterranean Vault (6:00–7:30)
- **Bedrock Vault:** `subterranean circular rotunda carved from volcanic basalt, flooded floor with two inches of mirror-black water, concentric aged-bronze gyroscopic rings rotating around a 20-foot suspended black-glass sphere.`
- **The Finger-Pattern Fissures:** `macro on the black sphere's glass surface: molten-gold fissures rearranging into the exact shape of two interlaced hands; Lucien and Elara looking down at matching golden marks glowing on their palms.`

### Chapter 6: The Golden Eye & The First Author (7:30–8:45)
- **Colossal Eye Reveal:** `extreme macro shot, deep in the dark interior behind the fractured black sphere, an immense ancient golden eye with an intelligent vertical iris slowly opens, wet cornea reflecting the tiny silhouettes of Lucien and Elara.`
- **Echoes of the First Pair:** `ethereal golden silhouettes visible inside the glass sphere, two figures from nine hundred years ago standing side by side, holding hands beneath alien skies.`

### Chapter 7: The Final Examination (8:45–9:40)
- **The Unfinished World:** `surreal landscape inside the sphere: a world half-created, stone cities that end abruptly in mid-air, rivers of light freezing in space, Lucien and Elara holding hands and drawing glowing golden lines that complete the landscape.`

### Chapter 8: The Unwritten (9:40–10:00)
- **The Decision to Leave:** `Lucien and Elara standing at the boundary of worlds, palms joined, leaving half of Lucien's pencil and Elara's drawing inside the sphere to complete the circuit, the golden eye closing in peace.`
- **Return to Classroom:** `normal dusk classroom, rain on the windows, Lucien and Elara seated at their desk, the pencil rolls across the wood and continues rolling naturally, on both their palms a faint permanent golden line remains visible.`
- **Title Card:** `black screen, a thin golden fissure draws THE UNWRITTEN.`
