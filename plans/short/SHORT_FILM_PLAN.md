The 10-minute film lives in `plans/full/`. The portal upload is a separate **58 s cut** ([COMPETITION_CUT_58s.md](COMPETITION_CUT_58s.md)).

## 1. Core story
**Title:** THE UNWRITTEN - A School Beyond Reality
**Premise:** Two students are pulled into a hidden academy beyond reality, where an ancient sealed creation recognises their inexplicable connection. Matching golden marks appear on their palms, and their arrival awakens something that should have stayed forgotten.
**Central mystery (never answered):** Are they connected across lifetimes, or did something inside the academy bring them together?
The audience must learn only: (1) they share an unusual connection, (2) the academy knows something about it, (3) their arrival woke a dangerous presence.

## 2. Relationship direction B (locked)
Drawn together without knowing why. Comfortable, curious, instinctively protective; **not** established as a couple. They feel something and don't yet understand it. Their bond grows through choices: in the final scene Elara hesitates and **Lucien deliberately takes her hand**.

## 4. Scene summaries
1. **The First Fracture.** Fade in; camera at the back of the class sweeps down and zooms out slightly; the bell rings; students pack and leave (about 5 s); Lucien and Elara stay. A teasing exchange. A pencil stops at the edge without falling; a golden crack opens in the shadow under their desk; the floor fractures.
2. **The World Beyond.** They fall through fragments of impossible worlds; hands slip, reconnect; they land on a black-stone causeway and release hands. The academy rises: floating bridges, a vast turning dome. The central doors open by themselves.
3. **The Forbidden Classroom.** Giant sketches and symbols; an unfinished humanoid drawing moves; the creature emerges, looks at their hands, a gold line crosses its body, it turns to a sealed door bearing the first fracture's symbol. Something moves behind it.
4. **The Chamber That Remembers.** The creature leads them into the chamber. A black-glass sphere in rotating rings. Gold fissures form the shape of their interlaced fingers; marks appear on both palms; the sphere shows silhouettes of them in unfamiliar worlds; the light dies and an enormous shape moves behind it.
5. **Something Woke Up.** The entity stirs; Elara reaches, hesitates; Lucien takes her hand; the marks join into one symbol; gold races through the academy; the dome stops; every window goes black; the entity opens one eye. Cut to black. Gold fissure. **THE UNWRITTEN.**

## 5. Visual identity (lock for every shot)
- **Characters:** fixed faces, hair, clothing, proportions. **One golden-mark design** everywhere.
- **Environment:** black stone, weathered bronze, teal stained glass, cold blue shadow, gold supernatural light, Gothic scale, black reflective surfaces, impossible geometry.
- **Camera:** wide for scale, close for emotion, slow controlled moves when exploring, faster only for fractures and the awakening.
- **Audio:** no narrator, sparse dialogue, original or licensed music, deliberate silence in the chamber.

## 6. How to generate
Never a whole scene in one clip. Order: character references -> environment references -> keyframes -> short animated clips (each shot is 2-6 s) -> continuity review -> edit -> sound -> export. **27 shots.**
**Focus Guard:** do not generate footage until this plan, `DIALOGUE.md` and the shot list agree.
