## 1. THE SECRET (the core that connects everything)

Nine hundred years ago, two students at the Meridian Academy — **the First Pair**, lovers — sat their final examination: *survive a world created by your own imagination.* They imagined a world together. It came alive, and it did not want to end. The Academy sealed it inside a black-glass sphere and rewrote the exam as a rule: **never again allow two who love each other to write together.** Teachers were taught that love makes creation dangerous. That is the lie the school is built on.

The sphere cannot be opened by force. It answers only to a pair bonded the way the First Pair were. As it weakens, it reaches across the boundary of worlds and calls **its pattern** — the shape of two joined hands. That is why the portal opens under a desk where the First Pair once sat (their carved initials are on it, with a tiny interlaced-hands mark), and why the sphere's fissures form **Lucien and Elara's own fingers.**

**The golden eye** is the surviving First Boy/Girl (ambiguous until late): trapped inside as the world's last author, holding it together. Not a monster. Watching two kids who look exactly like their memory.

**Meaning of the title:** *the unwritten* = a story nobody is allowed to finish for you. The Academy wants to write their ending. They refuse.

> Prophecy (inscription in the vault): **"Two hands wrote it. Two hands will close it. Neither will leave the same."**

## 2. CHARACTER BIBLE (draft — confirm names/faces)

**LUCIEN** — 16, tall, quiet, observant, protective by instinct but learning she doesn't need protecting. Charcoal school coat, old leather notebook he sketches in. Tell: touches his sleeve cuff when anxious.
**ELARA** — 16, sharp, brave, first to move toward the unknown. Charcoal coat, dark-teal scarf lining, pencil always behind her ear. Tell: tilts her head when she's figuring something out.
**Relationship rule:** affection is small and habitual (hand-holding, sleeve-catching), never announced. They never say "I love you" in the reel. Neither protects alone — when one steps in front, the other pulls them level.
**The Warden (movie only):** Headmistress ORVEN SELKE, calm, kind-looking, the one who believes the lie. Not a villain — a guardian who is wrong.
**The Eye:** see §1.

Visual rules: school uniform = charcoal long coat, dark-teal lining, bronze fasteners, geometric emblem. Palette: slate-teal shadows, 2700K golden magic, black-glass void. Magic is **gold only** (kintsugi cracks); never purple/blue sci-fi.

## 4. THE FULL FILM (~10 minutes) — "THE UNWRITTEN"

**Ch.1 — After the Bell (0:00–1:30)** = C1–C6 expanded: more of Lucien and Elara's ordinary day (she draws creatures in the margins of his notebook; he's afraid of leaving for a school abroad — stakes of separation). The initials on the desk. Portal, fall.

**Ch.2 — Meridian (1:30–3:00)** Landing, arrival. Students in teal-lined coats cross impossible bridges. A teacher finds them — not alarmed, but **relieved and horrified at once**: "You're early." The inscription: WELCOME TO YOUR REAL SCHOOL. They are told the portal under the desk only opens for "the Unlisted."

**Ch.3 — The Curriculum (3:00–4:30)** Four lessons with consequences, not montage:
- *Mathematics:* Lucien bends gravity — and discovers his equations only work when Elara's hand is on the dais. Instructor notices.
- *Science:* Elara ignites a galaxy; it brightens when Lucien walks into the room.
- *History:* A door steps them into a living lost city; Elara sees the First Pair's names carved in stone.
- *Art:* a drawing breathes — in Lucien's notebook margin, a creature Elara drew starts moving *without being finished.*

**Ch.4 — The Rule (4:30–6:00)** They learn the School's one unwritten rule: *paired creation is forbidden.* Headmistress Selke separates them — different towers. Each alone is ordinary. Alone, neither magic works. Intercut: lonely, parallel scenes; they find each other through a staircase that moves toward them.

**Ch.5 — The Vault (6:00–7:30)** Down past the restricted door. The sphere. Fissures form their hands. Marks on their palms. Selke arrives, not angry — afraid: "It's calling you to finish what was started." She tells the lie: *it will unmake everything.*

**Ch.6 — The Eye (7:30–8:45)** The sphere cracks. The golden eye opens, sees them — and does not threaten. A voice the audience hears only as the sound of the world: through the sphere's surface, tiny figures — the First Pair's hands, reaching. The truth: the eye is the last First Author, holding a world together alone for nine hundred years. Final exam begins: *survive a world created by your own imagination.*

**Ch.7 — The Exam (8:45–9:40)** They are pulled inside. A world half-written: cities that end mid-street, rivers that stop. They must **write** it — together, hands joined. Each creates something; the other fills in. The world stabilizes. The cost: the sphere asks for an author to stay as keeper. Selke's lie ends: the School chose to cage it rather than finish it.

**Ch.8 — Unwritten (9:40–10:00)** Lucien offers to stay; Elara refuses — pulls him level. They **rewrite the rule**: neither stays; they leave half their pencil/notebook inside as the world's third hand — the world now writes itself. The First Author's eye closes in peace. Back in the classroom at dusk: the desk is normal; the initials now read three pairs. Marks on palms remain. Pencil rolls — and doesn't stop.

Series hook: the Academy still has the Rule — and Selke saw everything.
