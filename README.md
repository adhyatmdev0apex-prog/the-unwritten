# THE UNWRITTEN — A School Beyond Reality

> Two students. One fracture beneath a classroom desk. An ancient academy above the clouds—and a sealed creation that recognizes the inexplicable pull between them.

*THE UNWRITTEN* is a photorealistic cinematic fantasy film produced in 9:16 vertical format.

---

## 1. Canonical Project Parameters (Locked)
- **Protagonists:** **Lucien** and **Elara**
- **Relationship Direction:** **B — Drawn together without knowing why** (comfortable, curious, instinctively protective; not established as already in love; hand-hold is a deliberate choice earned in Scene 5)
- **Narration:** None anywhere in the film
- **Canonical Short Film:** **1:50 (110 seconds)** across 5 scenes / 27 shots ([`plans/short/SHORT_FILM_PLAN.md`](plans/short/SHORT_FILM_PLAN.md))
- **Competition Deliverable:** **0:58 (58 seconds)** independently edited for the VIBGYOR AIVerse Creator Studio 45–60s rule ([`plans/short/COMPETITION_CUT_58s.md`](plans/short/COMPETITION_CUT_58s.md))
- **Extended Film Concept:** **~10:00** 6-act featurette screenplay kept separate in [`plans/full/`](plans/full/README.md)

## 3. Production Pipeline (4 Stages)
1. **Stage 1 — Character Identity:** Generate and approve Lucien (`B`) and Elara (`C`) master reference sheets and the palm-mark design before generating any scene clips.
2. **Stage 2 — World Identity:** Generate and approve the Gothic academy (`D`), forbidden classroom, chamber & sphere (`E`), and unfinished ink creature references.
3. **Stage 3 — Motion Tests:** Test 3 core shots first (Classroom sweep `Shot 01`, Academy reveal `Shot 06`, Sphere/hand interaction `Shot 08/09`) in Google Flow/Veo or Kling AI.
4. **Stage 4 — Full Production & Edit:** Generate remaining shots, edit the **1:50 canonical short film** and **58s competition cut** in DaVinci Resolve, mix the 6 audio tracks (`A1`–`A6`), and validate exports with FFmpeg.
