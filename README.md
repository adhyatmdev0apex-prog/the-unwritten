# THE UNWRITTEN

> Two students. One desk. A school that was never meant to be found.

An AI-made cinematic fantasy film. **Lucien** and **Elara** stay behind after the last bell. The floor opens, they fall together into a hidden academy above the clouds, and a sealed black-glass sphere answers to the shape of their joined hands.

- **Theme:** fantasy-land (VIBGYOR AIVerse Creator Studio, Team #58 "The Boys")
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, photoreal cinematic fantasy, **no narrator**
- **Deliverables:** 58-second competition cut + ~10-minute extended cut

## Locked rules

- Leads: **Lucien & Elara**, a boy and girl in love
- **No narrator anywhere.** Words come from the leads and from engraved/on-screen inscriptions
- They **fall** together (no stepping through the portal)
- Magic is **gold only** (kintsugi cracks)
- Competition cut stays within 45-60 s (target 58 s)
