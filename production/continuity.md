## 1. Relationship & Hand-Holding Continuity (Direction B — Strictly Enforced)

Lucien and Elara feel an inexplicable connection, but they are **not** established as already in love. Their hand-holding is earned in Act 4 as a conscious choice:

| Act / Sequence | Hand State | Continuity Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Act 1: The Last Bell (`S01-SH01` – `S01-SH03`)** | **Hands apart** | Sitting side by side at their wooden desk. Hands rest on books or lap. Zero hand-holding in the classroom. |
| **Act 2 Fall (`S02-SH01` – `S02-SH02`)** | **Survival catch** | In free fall, hands reach out, slip, Lucien catches Elara's wrist, and she grips his hand to stay together. |
| **Act 2 Landing & Reveal (`S02-SH02` – `S02-SH03`)** | **Hands apart** | Upon landing solidly on the black-stone platform, they **release hands immediately**. Hands hang at their sides during the academy reveal. |
| **Act 2 Exploration (`S02-SH04` – `S02-SH09`)** | **Hands apart** | Walking side by side exploring the enchanted classroom, library, and corridor. In `S02-SH05`, Elara reaches solo toward the floating equation. |
| **Act 3: The Chamber (`S03-SH01` – `S03-SH04`)** | **Hands apart (open palms)** | Hands remain separate even when golden fissures form interlaced fingers and matching marks appear on their open palms (`S03-SH02`). |
| **Act 4: The Climax (`S04-SH01` – `S04-SH04`)** | **Hesitation → Deliberate Choice** | Elara reaches out and **hesitates** (`S04-SH01`). Lucien deliberately **takes her hand** (*"Whatever that thing is… we face it together."*). Marks connect into one continuous loop, and hands stay joined through the eye reveal (`S04-SH04`). |

- **Lucien:** ~16 years old, tall and lean, dark tousled hair, calm observant dark eyes. Tailored charcoal school coat with subtle dark-teal inner lining and minimal aged-bronze fasteners over a dark uniform. Carries an old leather notebook.
- **Elara:** ~16 years old, intelligent observant expression, natural understated look. Tailored charcoal school coat with visible dark-teal scarf lining over a dark uniform. Small hexagonal wooden pencil behind her ear.

## 3. Environment & Architectural Continuity

1. **Ordinary School Classroom (Act 1):**
   - Natural warm late-afternoon sunlight through tall windows on the left.
   - Lucien and Elara's desk remains mid-room.
   - The thin golden crack beneath the desk in `S01-SH03` is the only supernatural light in Act 1.
2. **The Gothic Academy Exterior (Act 2 & Act 4):**
   - Monumental black basalt architecture, aged bronze mechanisms, tall teal stained-glass windows, and an enormous rotating central dome.
   - `S04-SH03` (dome stops, windows turn black) must match the silhouette from `S02-SH03`.
3. **The Enchanted Classroom (Act 2):**
   - Floating wooden desks, celestial constellation blackboard, spun-gold mathematical equations drifting in mid-air, miniature rotating solar system above teacher's desk.
   - When the academy awakens in `S04-SH02`, this exact room freezes mid-air.
4. **The Library Between Worlds (Act 2):**
   - Multi-tiered library with orbiting leather books around a central spiral staircase, floating platforms, and a massive galaxy rose window.
   - In `S04-SH02`, the orbiting books freeze instantly in space.
5. **The Forbidden Corridor (Act 2):**
   - Arched stone hallway with framed portraits covered in black shroud cloth.
   - Wall lanterns click off sequentially into darkness behind them.
   - Massive 20-foot blackened iron door at the end etched with the golden fracture symbol.
6. **The Forbidden Chamber (Acts 3 & 4):**
   - Subterranean circular basalt chamber, flooded mirror-black stone floor, 20-foot suspended black-glass sphere ringed by rotating bronze gyroscopic rings.
   - The darkness behind the sphere conceals a colossal presence; only a massive golden eye opens in `S04-SH04`. Never show a full monster body.
