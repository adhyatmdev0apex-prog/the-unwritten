# Continuity

## Fixed in the classroom (Act I / C1-C5)
- Desk position and window side never change. The portal desk is at the **back** of the room; the characters retreat to the **front**.
- Light: cool dusk from the windows, one flickering fluorescent. Gold appears **only** from the fissures.
- Pencil: hexagonal, rolls toward the desk edge and stops.
- The same notebook (leather) stays with Lucien.

## Characters
- Lucien: tall, structured charcoal coat, dark-teal lining, leather notebook.
- Elara: charcoal coat, teal scarf lining, pencil behind ear.
- Hands: they are **joined from C4 onward** and stay joined through the fall (slipping only once). Marks appear on **both palms at the same place** in C10.
- The same fingers must interlace in C9 (sphere pattern) and C10 (palm mark).

## Gold motif (must repeat)
Kintsugi fissure: C4 desk crack, C5 tear, C9 sphere, C10 palm line, title. Same look each time.

## Sound continuity
Pencil roll opens and closes the film.

- [ ] Faces match references
- [ ] Coat and scarf details match
- [ ] Room layout and light direction match
- [ ] Hands correct (five fingers, right grip)
- [ ] No unintended magic or glow
