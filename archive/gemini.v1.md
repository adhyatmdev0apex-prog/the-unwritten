THE UNWRITTEN
VISION DRAFT 1.5 — THE COMPLETE VISUAL EXPERIENCE (ENHANCED)
Genre: Photorealistic cinematic fantasy mystery

Initial development cut: Approximately 90 seconds

Competition cut: 45–60 seconds

Format: Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920)

Visual direction: Grounded physical realism, localized violations of physics, restrained molten-gold magic, monumental weathered architecture.

Protagonists (100% Original Fictional Cast):

Kael (Student 1): 16, sharp angular features, dark hair with a faint silver streak, observant, wearing a tailored charcoal school coat.

Veyron (Student 2): 16, taller, tousled obsidian hair, carrying an old leather-bound sketchpad and notebook.

The experience we want
The audience begins in a tactile, ordinary place they immediately recognize.

A tiny law of physics behaves incorrectly.

The characters discover the anomaly—not with panic, but with quiet fascination.

They cross a physical boundary into a world that should not exist.

They experience architectural and scientific wonder.

Then they discover that the wonder was built to hide something ancient and dangerous.

The film cuts to black at the exact second the audience realizes the true purpose of the school.

ACT I — SOMETHING IS WRONG
SCENE 01 — THE LAST CLASSROOM
Time: 00:00–00:05

What you see
Black screen.
A faint 60Hz fluorescent hum.
The dry scrape of a wooden chair pushed back against linoleum.
Cut to a realistic school classroom at late dusk.
The camera faces diagonally across the room, utilizing the vertical 9:16 height to show both the scarred wooden desks in the lower foreground and the tall, rain-streaked windows reaching toward the ceiling in the upper frame. Empty chairs and forgotten canvas schoolbags sit in the middle distance. Outside, the sky is cooling into deep slate blue.
The classroom feels unmistakably ordinary.
Two fictional students—Kael and Veyron—remain near a back row desk.
Veyron is zipping his bag shut. Kael is glancing toward the darkened corridor outside the doorway.
Overhead, a fluorescent tube flickers once—a brief, cold stutter of light.
Neither boy reacts.
The camera begins a nearly imperceptible forward push.
The room feels quiet, heavy with after-school stillness. Somewhere far down the hall, distant footsteps fade away.

What the characters do
Veyron fastens the buckle on his bag.
Kael shifts a heavy textbook aside to pick up a graphite pencil.
His fingers brush the pencil; it rolls a few inches across the scarred wood toward the edge of the desk.
Everything obeys normal physics.

Camera
A controlled, steady dolly push forward on a 35mm lens.
Zero handheld shake. No artificial lens flares or fantasy filters.
The viewer should feel physically standing inside the room.

Sound

Heavy, realistic classroom room tone.

Faint, echoing footsteps receding down a tiled hallway.

Coarse canvas fabric and a metallic zipper closing.

The hollow wooden roll of a hexagonal pencil.

A low, persistent electrical ballast hum.
No musical score yet.

What the audience thinks
This is a grounded, real-world film. Nothing supernatural has happened—which makes the room feel vulnerable.

SCENE 02 — THE FIRST IMPOSSIBILITY
Time: 00:05–00:09

What you see
Macro close-up of the pencil reaching the bevelled edge of the wooden desk.
Half of its lead tip overhangs the drop.
And then it stops.
Not because it hits an obstruction. It simply locks in place, defying momentum.
The camera holds perfectly still on the suspended tip.
Beside it, a loose sheet of lined notebook paper begins to slide—not blown by wind, but pulled smoothly across the wood grain.
It reaches the edge, folds downward at a crisp angle, and slips toward the floor.
The camera tilts smoothly downward, tracking the falling sheet.
Directly beneath the desk lies a patch of shadow.
At first glance, it reads as ordinary dusk shade cast by the desk panel.
Then the eye registers the error: the darkness is completely devoid of texture.
Where dusty floorboards and the back legs of the desk should be faintly visible, a small region of the floor has ceased to reflect light entirely.
Surrounding grey room shadows begin to bend inward, stretching slowly toward that central black point like iron filings toward a magnet.

Physical details
The wooden desk legs remain completely solid and undisturbed.
The paper slides across the floorboards with genuine physical friction before touching the dark boundary.
Microscopic dust motes in the dusk light stop drifting randomly and begin pulling downward in straight, parallel lines.
The rest of the classroom lighting remains completely untouched.
This is not a bombastic explosion of magic; it is a quiet, localized failure of reality.

Camera
Start in tight macro on the pencil, pan slightly to the sliding paper, and tilt vertically downward to frame the pitch-black anomaly at the bottom of the 9:16 screen.

Sound
The wooden rattle of the pencil cuts off mid-roll with unnatural precision.
The high-frequency buzz of the fluorescent lights is sucked away, leaving a sudden acoustic vacuum.
Beneath human hearing, a deep 30Hz sub-bass pressure begins to vibrate.
The dry whisper of paper scraping against wood.
Then, emerging from the floor itself, a hollow, cavernous draft—like wind passing over the mouth of a bottomless well.

What the audience thinks
That shadow isn't a lack of light. It has mass—and it is pulling things in.

SCENE 03 — THE DARKNESS MOVES
Time: 00:09–00:14

What you see
Low-angle vertical shot placed directly on the classroom floor.
The Vantablack patch expands outward from beneath the desk like spilled ink, yet it has no wet surface.
As its perimeter advances across the floor, the floorboards aren't broken or burned—instead, all specular reflection simply vanishes wherever the boundary passes.
A pale rectangle of window light on the floor is eaten away inch by inch.
The underside of the wooden desk dissolves into indistinct blackness.
At the upper edge of the frame, Kael and Veyron’s shoes step into view.
Kael freezes mid-stride as the dark boundary stops two inches from the sole of his boot.
Veyron takes a slow, instinctive half-step backward.
The fallen sheet of notebook paper lifts three centimeters off the floor.
It does not flutter chaotically; it rises flat, tilts on an invisible axis, and glides silently into the dark patch—disappearing the instant it crosses the threshold.

What must remain physically convincing
There is no cyclone wind in the room yet. The paper’s movement is surgical, slow, and governed by an alien gravity.
Neither boy screams or jumps back like a cartoon. Their body language is tense, quiet, and hyper-focused—trying to process a visual error their brains cannot explain.

Camera
A slow, creeping lateral slider shot at floor level, keeping the dark anomaly dominant in the lower foreground while the boys’ legs anchor the upper frame.

Sound

An uneven, Geiger-like sub-sonic tremor in the floorboards.

A single crisp snap of paper tension as the sheet lifts.

A faint, singing metallic overtone—like a struck tuning fork held against stone.

The sound of both boys holding their breath.

What the audience thinks
There is no floor underneath that desk anymore.

SCENE 04 — THE FIRST GOLDEN FISSURE
Time: 00:14–00:19

What you see
Extreme macro close-up looking straight down into the pitch-black void.
For one full second, the frame is absolute black.
Then, without warning, a microscopic hair-line fracture of molten gold scores across the center.
It looks like a crack in black obsidian glass—except there is no solid surface there to crack.
The primary line splinters into two sharp, geometric branches, like golden lightning frozen in mid-strike or Japanese kintsugi repair lines.
A second fracture opens parallel to the first.
The golden light is dense, liquid, and contained. It does not flood the classroom with blinding bloom.
Instead, it behaves like real firelight through a keyhole: casting a sharp, warm amber rim-light onto the splintered wood under the desk and the leather toes of Kael’s shoes.
The fissures flex slightly—as if an immense weight on the other side is pressing upward against the fabric of the room.
A single ember-like mote of golden dust drifts out of the crack, rises two inches, and dissolves.
The entire dark region pulses once in unison.

Camera
A slow, hypnotic vertical push straight down toward the golden fracture.
The camera stops just short of crossing it, forcing the viewer to inspect the texture of the light.

Lighting
The golden fissure becomes the sole key light in the shot.
Light falloff obeys the inverse-square law: warm and bright on the underside of the desk, faint and bronze on the boys' cuffs, leaving the upper classroom in cold dusk teal.

Sound
A razor-sharp, crystalline plink—like thick winter ice cracking across a frozen lake.
Immediately answered by a deep, subterranean bronze resonance.
Then a single, heavy low-end pulse—thump-thump—indistinguishable from a slow heartbeat.

What the audience thinks
This isn't a glitch. It’s a sealed boundary—and something on the other side is unlocking it.

SCENE 05 — THE STUDENTS SEE IT
Time: 00:19–00:24

What you see
Cut to a medium two-shot of Kael and Veyron, shooting upward from floor level through the gap beneath the desk.
Veyron remains standing, his grip tightening on the strap of his leather bag.
Kael slowly lowers himself to one knee to peer beneath the desk.
Moving ribbons of warm golden light reflect across their corneas and trace the contours of their cheekbones.
Their expressions are grounded and micro-detailed: parted lips, unblinking eyes, the quiet adrenaline of witnessing the impossible.
Veyron extends his hand a few inches toward Kael’s shoulder—a silent, protective instinct to pull him back.
Kael doesn't feel it; he leans an inch closer to the edge.
Cut to a tight over-the-shoulder shot past Kael’s temple looking down.
Between the widening golden fissures, the blackness no longer looks flat. It has terrifying parallax depth—dropping away for miles beneath the floorboards of a second-story classroom.

Character behaviour
Zero melodramatic acting. Stillness sells the realism.
Kael speaks barely above a breath, his voice dry:
KAEL: "Tell me you're seeing that."
Veyron doesn't take his eyes off the floor:
VEYRON: "I'm trying not to."

Sound
The sub-bass rumble deepens, subtly rattling a metal ruler on a nearby desk.
Outside, a distant car horn or bird call from the ordinary world fades away completely, signaling that the room is detaching from normal space.

What the audience thinks
Both of them see it. There is no turning back to an ordinary afternoon now.

ACT II — THE PORTAL OPENS
SCENE 06 — REALITY BEGINS TO BREAK
Time: 00:24–00:29

What you see
Return to a wider vertical view of the anomaly.
The golden fissures accelerate, spider-webbing outward across the darkness.
The black boundary breaks free from beneath the desk, climbing vertically up the empty air of the classroom like an invisible monolith tearing open from the inside.
Its edges are jagged and architectural—not a spinning sci-fi circle, but a structural rift in three-dimensional space.
The wooden desk groans as its shadow stretches all the way up the back wall.
Three sheets of notebook paper and chalk dust lift into the air, orbiting slowly in a calm, zero-gravity suspension around the tear.
Suddenly, every golden fracture line connects into a complex, interlocking sacred-geometry lattice covering the entire vertical rift.
For half a second, the lattice flares bright amber—then the golden lines retract to the outer perimeter like a lock disengaging.
The center drops open.

Critical visual idea
The portal must feel architectural and structural—as if the classroom were painted on thin canvas and someone just parted the seams.
Sequence of perception: 1. Vantablack void → 2. Geometric gold lattice → 3. Dimensional window.

Camera
A slow, steady dolly backward, allowing the vertical tear to rise and claim the full central spine of the 9:16 frame, with Kael and Veyron framed as dark silhouettes on either side.

Sound
The low rumble swells into the sound of tectonic stone grinding against bronze gears.
A deep, harmonic chord of cellos and low brass enters underneath the sound effects, building tension without overpowering the room noises.

SCENE 07 — THE WORLD BEYOND
Time: 00:29–00:34

What you see
Inside the vertical tear, the blackness clears like dense night mist parting.
It does not shimmer like water; it simply reveals clear, cold air on the other side.
First, distant pinpoints of warm amber lantern light appear in the deep background.
Then, emerging out of banks of deep teal storm clouds, colossal architectural silhouettes take shape.
A monumental Gothic academy stands suspended over a bottomless chasm of sky.
Weathered black-stone spires rise higher than the top of the portal frame.
Slender stone aqueducts and arched bridges span between floating mountain crags above cascading sky-waterfalls.
High above one tower, a colossal bronze astrolabe ring turns slowly through the cloud layer—moving so gradually that the viewer realizes the ring must be thousands of feet wide.
In the foreground, the dusty wooden desks of the ordinary classroom sit only three feet away from a drop into an endless sky.

Composition (3-Layer Depth Lock)

Foreground: Scratched wooden classroom desks, floating chalk dust, and the dark silhouettes of Kael and Veyron.

Middle ground: The jagged, glowing molten-gold perimeter of the spatial tear.

Background: Miles of volumetric teal clouds and the towering, warm-lit Gothic spires of the Academy.

Camera
A slow pedestal rise behind the boys' shoulders, tilting slightly to emphasize the staggering vertical height of the distant towers through the classroom rift.

Voice-over
A weathered, calm, resonant voice speaks—intimate rather than booming:
NARRATOR (V.O.): "Beyond the world you were taught to accept..."
(A two-second beat as a distant bell tolls inside the portal)
NARRATOR (V.O.): "...lies the place where the laws are written."

Sound
Cold, high-altitude mountain wind spills out of the portal into the warm classroom.
A single, deep cathedral bell rings from miles away inside the rift, its metallic echo rolling across the clouds.
The orchestral strings open into a wide, awe-inspiring minor chord.

What the audience thinks
That isn't an illusion or a projection. There is real air, real stone, and an entire civilization waiting on the other side of that desk.

SCENE 08 — THE DECISION
Time: 00:34–00:38

What you see
Medium reverse-angle shot looking from inside the portal boundary back at Kael and Veyron standing in the classroom.
Warm golden light from the rift edges and cool teal sky-light illuminate the fronts of their faces and coats; behind them, the ordinary classroom looks small, flat, and desaturated.
In the reflection of Kael’s eyes, clouds drift past ancient towers.
The draft from the other world stirs their hair and the hems of their dark blazers.
Kael takes one deliberate step toward the threshold.
Veyron glances back over his shoulder at the classroom door—the last tether to their normal life.
He hesitates for half a second, then looks back at Kael.
Kael doesn't give a speech; he simply tilts his head subtly toward the opening and holds Veyron’s gaze.
Veyron nods once, tightens his grip on his leather notebook, and steps up beside him.

Camera
Slow backward tracking shot pulling deeper into the portal threshold, drawing the boys—and the audience—forward toward the edge.

Sound
Two crisp footsteps on wooden floorboards.
The wind rises in volume, carrying the faint, distant murmur of a massive hall.
The orchestral strings hold a tense, shimmering high harmonic note.

What the audience thinks
They aren't being kidnapped or pulled against their will. They are choosing to cross over.

SCENE 09 — CROSSING THE BOUNDARY
Time: 00:38–00:43

What you see
Tight tracking shot at chest-and-hand level as the boys reach the jagged golden edge.
Kael raises his right hand and pushes his fingertips two inches past the boundary plane.
Instantly, the physics of light on his skin change: the flat classroom dusk vanishes on his fingers, replaced by crisp, cold mountain sky-light and fine condensation mist collecting on his knuckles.
He pulls his hand back half an inch, watches the light shift across his skin, then exhales.
He steps squarely across the threshold.
Veyron follows immediately at his shoulder.
The camera glides directly behind their backs through the narrow golden seam.
As the lens passes the plane of the rift, the molten-gold fracture lines flare around the outer border of the 9:16 screen, followed by a 6-frame pass through dense obsidian shadow.

Important physical rule
No swirling purple vortex or warping tunnel. Crossing the portal is like stepping through an invisible doorway from a stuffy room directly onto the ledge of a high mountain cathedral.

Sound

Footstep 1: Hollow wooden classroom floorboard.

Footstep 2: Wet, solid, echoing black basalt stone in open air.
At the exact frame the camera crosses the seam, all music and classroom room-tone drop to a momentary acoustic zero—before the open sky rushes in.

ACT III — THE ACADEMY
SCENE 10 — THE FIRST STEP INTO ANOTHER WORLD
Time: 00:43–00:47

What you see
Emerging from the shadow transition, Kael and Veyron come to a halt on a vast causeway of weathered black basalt stone.
The camera sits just behind their shoulders.
Neither boy moves a muscle.
Stretching ahead of them is a colossal, rain-dampened stone bridge leading to the main gate of the Academy.
Towering pointed Gothic arches—carved with geometric bronze reliefs rather than gargoyles—frame an ascending grand staircase that vanishes into drifting cloud banks.
Above the mist, the central Citadel Spire pierces the upper third of the vertical frame.
Every material looks centuries old and physically heavy: pitted volcanic stone, oxidized bronze lanterns swaying in the wind, shallow puddles on the flagstones reflecting the warm amber glow of the interior halls.
Thousands of feet below the bridge parapets, an ocean of deep teal clouds rolls slowly over submerged mountain peaks.
Far up on a secondary viaduct, tiny robed figures walk between towers—giving the architecture a staggering human scale reference.

Camera
Starts at a grounded human eye-level behind Kael and Veyron, then executes a slow, majestic crane rise upward and slightly backward—shrinking the two boys at the bottom of the frame while the impossible height of the Academy fills the vertical canvas.

Lighting
Dual-temperature contrast: cold, diffused slate-teal skylight from the clouds above versus rich, inviting 2700K golden lantern light spilling out of the grand entrance arches.

Sound

Open, high-altitude wind whistling softly through carved stone arches.

Distant, heavy bronze bells tolling in slow counterpoint.

The wet crunch-echo of their final step on damp stone.

The main musical motif swells to its full, sweeping orchestral statement—deep choir, solo cello, and French horns.

What the audience thinks
This world has history, weight, and scale on the level of a feature film.

SCENE 11 — THE ACADEMY REVEAL
Time: 00:47–00:52

What you see
An expansive interior-to-exterior architectural gliding shot.
The camera wipes past a massive, moss-veined obsidian pillar to reveal the inner sanctum of the Academy complex.
Multiple tiered sanctuaries and library towers hang suspended at different altitudes across the chasm, linked by slender stone bridges and rotating bronze rings.
Atop the eastern wing, a split-hemisphere astronomical observatory dome rotates on heavy brass tracks.
Through tall stained-glass lancet windows, shafts of volumetric golden sunlight cut diagonally through the blue mist.
In the middle distance, a 40-foot stone staircase detaches smoothly from its lower landing, pivots on a central golden fulcrum, and locks into an upper gallery with quiet mechanical weight.
The motion is unhurried and utilitarian—as if the building itself is an enormous, living clockwork instrument.

Camera
A smooth, lateral parallax dolly move emerging from behind the foreground column, followed by a gentle upward tilt—never spinning fast, letting the eye drink in the detail.

Sound
The score remains expansive and wondrous.
Underneath, the subtle mechanical thrum of distant rotating brass gears, the distant murmur of echoes in stone corridors, and the low thunk of the stone staircase locking into place.

Voice-over
NARRATOR (V.O.): "A sanctuary where the impossible is not forbidden..."
(Beat)
NARRATOR (V.O.): "...It is the curriculum."

ACT IV — WHAT DO THEY TEACH HERE?
(Note: All three disciplines play in the 90-second cut; for the 45–60s competition cut, these three shots can either play as rapid 3.5-second rhythmic cuts or focus on Scenes 12 & 14).

SCENE 12 — MATHEMATICS: THE ARCHITECTURE OF GRAVITY
Time: 00:52–00:56

What you see
Inside a soaring, open-vaulted lecture hall.
In the center of a circular stone dais inlaid with concentric brass rings, a fractured two-ton sphere of raw dark granite hovers four feet off the floor.
No glowing smoke holds it up—only crisp, razor-thin three-dimensional geometric rings and calculus vectors made of spun golden light rotating silently in the air around it.
At the edge of the platform, Kael raises one hand tentatively, mimicking the gesture of an unseen instructor.
As he tilts his wrist by thirty degrees, a corresponding golden vector ring shifts its angle.
With heavy, believable physical inertia, the two-ton stone sphere stops spinning horizontally and begins a slow, majestic vertical orbit around a central brass spire.
Three smaller granite chips lift from the floor and lock into satellite orbits around the main boulder, casting sharp, moving shadows across the inlaid floor.

Camera
Medium-wide vertical framing capturing the inlaid floor at the bottom, Kael in the middle ground, and the heavy orbiting boulder rising into the upper frame during a slow push-in.

Sound

The deep, sub-audible hum of compressed air and heavy granite mass moving through space.

A clean, crystalline glass-harmonica chime as the golden geometric ring locks its angle.

What the audience learns
In this academy, equations aren't written on chalkboards—they are built in three-dimensional space to command gravity itself.

SCENE 13 — SCIENCE: A GALAXY IN A CLASSROOM
Time: 00:56–01:00

What you see
Cut to a darkened, amphitheater-style astronomical laboratory.
Above an ancient dark-slate laboratory table hovers a spherical refractive containment field—looking like high-density optical crystal rather than a soap bubble, bending the light of the room around its edges.
Inside the field, cold dark dust clouds collapse inward toward a central point.
A silent, pinpoint flash of blinding white-gold light ignites at the core.
From that core, delicate spiral arms of violet nebula gas and thousands of microscopic, pinpoint stars unfurl in slow rotation—a living, four-foot-wide spiral galaxy suspended inside a classroom.
Veyron stands close to the containment boundary, his face and dark eyes illuminated by the rotating purple and golden starlight reflecting across the curved optical field.

Physical realism
The light from the miniature galaxy must interact physically with the room: illuminating dust motes on Veyron’s coat sleeves, reflecting off brass calipers on the table, and showing true three-dimensional depth inside the spiral arms.

Camera
Tight, intimate slow push-in past the curved edge of the containment field toward Veyron’s awe-struck eyes.

Sound

A soft inward vacuum whoomp as the core ignites.

A shimmering, granular synthesizer pad layered with faint electrostatic crackles.

A warm, deep bass pedal note as the spiral galaxy stabilizes.

What the audience learns
Creation itself is studied and replicated here—which plants the quiet question: what happens if a creation grows too large to contain?

SCENE 14 — ART: THE DRAWING THAT BREATHES
Time: 01:00–01:05

What you see
Extreme macro shot looking across a heavy sheet of black-dyed vellum parchment resting on an angled drafting table.
Drawn across the dark parchment in metallic gold and charcoal ink is an anatomical illustration of an unfamiliar winged creature—part avian, part scaled serpent, rendered in exquisite Da Vinci-style linework.
As the camera watches, a single curved golden line along the creature’s ribcage shifts outward by two millimeters.
It expands, then contracts—the drawing is breathing.
The metallic ink lifts off the flat paper fibers, thickening into three-dimensional, overlapping obsidian scales with golden edges.
The creature’s sculpted head rises half an inch above the surface of the page.
Its eyelid parts, revealing a tiny, wet, amber eye that catches the room’s lantern light.
One miniature feathered wing unfolds upward out of the flat ink lines, casting a crisp, real-world shadow back down across the un-animated sketch on the parchment.

Camera
100mm macro lens positioned nearly parallel to the paper surface, pulling back slowly as the creature’s head and wing rise into three-dimensional focus.

Sound

The dry, fibrous scratch of metallic ink separating from heavy parchment.

A tiny, leathery creak as the wing unfolds.

A single, delicate intake of breath.

What the audience learns
The barrier between a thought on paper and a living, breathing entity has been erased. The wonder has reached its peak—and now the tone turns.

ACT V — THE WARNING
SCENE 15 — THE RESTRICTED CHAMBER
Time: 01:05–01:10

What you see
The warm golden glow of the upper classrooms vanishes.
Cut to the deep foundations beneath the Academy, where rough-hewn black bedrock meets monumental brutalist stone.
Kael and Veyron stand small before a thirty-foot vault door forged from blackened iron.
Etched deep into the metal is a massive bipartite seal: on the left, a perfectly symmetrical geometric polyhedron; on the right, an uncontained, jagged spiral.
A hairline seam of pale gold runs down the center of the seal.
With slow, hydraulic weight, the twin iron doors part inward.
Through the widening gap, an immense subterranean rotunda comes into view—its floor flooded with two inches of still, mirror-black water.
Suspended at the exact geometric center of the chamber hangs a twenty-foot sphere of flawless, light-absorbing black glass.
Three colossal bronze gyroscopic rings orbit the sphere at different velocities.
Inlaid into the flooded stone floor below, channels of golden light flow inward toward the base of the rings—not drawing power from the sphere, but actively pumping energy in to keep it locked shut.

Environment
Every angle of the chamber’s architecture—buttresses, chains, and floor conduits—points inward toward the sphere like a high-security containment bunker built by ancient masters.

Camera
Symmetrical, centered one-point perspective shot starting behind Kael and Veyron’s shoulders and gliding slowly through the parting iron doors toward the suspended black sphere.

Sound
All orchestral warmth cuts out.
Only the deep, rhythmic, industrial thrum-clank of the massive rotating bronze rings and the hollow echo of water dripping onto flooded stone.

What the audience thinks
This is not a classroom or a library. This entire school was built on top of a vault.

SCENE 16 — THE OBJECT RECOGNISES THEM
Time: 01:10–01:15

What you see
Medium close-up on the black-glass sphere and its orbiting bronze rings.
As Kael and Veyron’s boots step across the threshold into the shallow black water, the outer bronze ring suddenly shudders—and slams to a dead halt.
A second later, the middle ring locks in place with a shower of tiny bronze sparks.
Along the flooded floor, the golden conduit lines flare from calm amber to unstable white-gold.
At the edge of the platform, Kael stops.
Across the mirror-smooth, pitch-black surface of the suspended sphere, a razor-thin golden fissure cracks open.
Then a second branches off it.
It is the exact same kintsugi fracture geometry that opened beneath their classroom desk in Scene 04.
Cut to the boys: the realization hits their faces simultaneously.
Kael involuntarily lifts his hand halfway toward the sphere; Veyron’s hand shoots out and grips Kael’s forearm, stopping him cold.

Camera
Tight shot on the locking rings, quick cut to the boys' frozen reaction, then push back in on the black sphere as the familiar golden fracture network spreads across its equator.

Sound

Two deafening, echoing metallic brake locks (CLANG—CLANG).

Total mechanical silence as the rings stop.

Then the exact same crystalline ice-crack tone and slow heartbeat pulse from the classroom floor—now ten times larger.

Narrative significance
The visual and audio callback connects the entire film in a single second: whatever is locked inside this vault is the thing that opened the portal under their desk and brought them here.

SCENE 17 — THE EYE
Time: 01:15–01:20

What you see
Extreme close-up on the center of the twenty-foot black-glass sphere.
The golden fissures converge and widen into a vertical aperture.
A single jagged shard of black glass detaches from the surface and drifts outward in slow motion, weightless.
Through the gap, we do not see the back wall of the cavern—we see an impossibly vast, dark dimensional expanse inside the sphere.
Something monumental shifts in that interior darkness.
Then, filling the entire height of the fracture, a colossal golden eye opens.
It is hyper-realistic, ancient, and terrifying in scale—its wet cornea reflecting the tiny, distorted silhouettes of Kael and Veyron standing on the flooded floor below, its iris composed of shifting, microscopic rings of light.
The eye does not thrash or roar.
It simply focuses downward with devastating intelligence, looking directly at the two boys it summoned.
The final inner bronze ring around the sphere begins to spin backward at violent speed.

Important creative decision
Never show the full creature. Seeing only a massive, intelligent eye peering through a six-inch crack in a twenty-foot sphere is infinitely more cinematic and haunting than a full CGI monster.

Camera
A slow, relentless push straight toward the vertical crack. The moment the eyelid parts and locks focus on the lens, the camera freezes dead in its tracks.

Sound
The music drops out completely.
A sub-audible 20Hz pressure drop—like the air being pulled out of the theater.
A single, wet, resonant thrum as the eye focuses, followed by dead silence.

SCENE 18 — THE REVELATION
Time: 01:20–01:25

What you see
Cut to a tight two-shot of Kael and Veyron standing in the dark water.
The rest of the subterranean cavern has fallen into pitch blackness; the only light in the frame is the fierce, undulating golden glow cast by the giant eye in front of them, etching sharp highlights across their wet shoes and pale faces.
Kael stares upward, his voice barely a whisper:
KAEL: "This isn't a school."
Veyron’s eyes stay locked on the golden reflection in front of them:
VEYRON: "Then what is it?"
Before the last syllable finishes echoing, the pupil of the colossal eye contracts—and the sphere emits a blinding, silent shockwave of golden light that rushes straight toward the camera.
SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

Sound
The whispered dialogue is recorded bone-dry and intimate against total silence.
As the shockwave hits, a massive, distorted brass and sub-bass impact (BRAAAM) explodes and cuts off on a razor edge the exact frame the screen goes black.

What the audience understands
The Academy wasn't built to teach students magic—it was built as a containment system, and Kael and Veyron were brought here by the thing inside the sphere.
